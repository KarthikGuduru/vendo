## Facts and constraints (checked 12 Oct 2026)

- Trustified is an independent lab-testing certification. The only certified
  product confirmed in research was Avvatar whey (Trustified Gold). No evidence
  that Coke Zero, Sprite Zero, Diet Coke, Paper Boat Zero, Epigamia or SuperYou
  snacks hold a Trustified certificate.
- So the page states the *stocking rule* ("it passes Trustified's independent
  lab tests, or it does not get a slot"), and shelf labels name categories, not
  brands. No page copy implies any named brand is certified or partnered.
- "Thaali protein nachos" could not be found. Nearest real match: Taali
  (protein puffs, makhana). Not named on the page.
- No real numbers exist yet (no machines placed, no revenue, no catalogue
  size). Therefore: no counters, no stats, no prices.
- Generated imagery shows plain, unbranded packaging. No real brand packaging
  is imitated.

## Positioning line

"Press any button." The iPod move: not "a curated low-GI catalogue across five
categories", but the single consequence: every button is a good choice.

## Journey

1. Spark: a lit machine in a dark office. Press any button.
2. Recognition: today's office snack is a choice between what you want and
   what you'll regret.
3. Turn: we did the second-guessing. One stocking rule.
4. Range (peak): five shelves, top to bottom, each one a good choice.
5. Agency: not on the shelf? Scan, ask, it's in by next week.
6. Commitment: put one in your office.

## Feeling curve

```
1  Curiosity   a single glowing machine in a dark office, leaves in the near dark, the glass catching light
2  Recognition the pantry under a flat tube light, biscuits and a sugary cola, lines that land a little too close
3  Relief      the tube light wipes away to clean machine light, one sentence of rule
4  Delight     the camera drops into the glass and walks down the shelves, codes ticking on the LED, each row landing whole
5  Agency      a real field: type what you want, a restock ticket prints with next week's date on it
6  Resolve     the delivery tray, one object waiting in it, one line, one button
```

## The peak

"It scrolls you right into the vending machine and walks you down every shelf,
and the keypad on the side actually works."

Lives in act 4. Largest span on the page. Act 3 is the quiet before it: type on
a plain ground, no imagery motion.

can press any button on its keypad and it's always a good pick.
